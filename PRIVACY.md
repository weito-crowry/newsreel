# Privacy Policy — newsreel

Last updated: 2026-10-25

## About this app

"newsreel" is a personal, self-hosted tool operated by its owner. It is not a
public service and is not offered to third parties.

## Data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app uses the Google Sheets API
(`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets`) to read and update a single
job-management spreadsheet owned by the owner: article IDs, statuses, node
names, timestamps, and local output paths related to the owner's own
news-video production workflow.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to coordinate the owner's own video-generation jobs
across the owner's own computers. It is stored only in the owner's
spreadsheet and on the owner's own machines.

## Data sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or
analytics. No data leaves the owner's Google account and the owner's own
machines, except through Google APIs operated under the owner's own
authorization.

## Credentials

OAuth tokens are stored only in local files on the owner's own computers.
The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google Account
"third-party access" settings, which immediately stops all access.
